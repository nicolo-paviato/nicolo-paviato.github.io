I am a postdoctoral researcher in mathematics at the Weizmann Institute of Science, working with [Prof. Omri Sarig](https://www.weizmann.ac.il/math/sarigo/home). Prior to this, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem with [Prof. Mike Hochman](http://www.ma.huji.ac.il/~mhochman/). I received my PhD in 2023 from the University of Warwick, under the supervision of [Prof. Ian Melbourne](https://math-ianmelbourne.github.io).

Research
======
My research focuses on the statistical properties of deterministic systems, with a particular emphasis on the central limit theorem and its functional counterpart. More recently, I have also explored the typical behavior of random sequences of independently drawn digits in connection with Poisson genericity.
